Arduino
Robotic Arm

Four servos. Two joysticks. One Uno. Watch the arm pick a part and place it, then scroll to see how every joint works.

View on GitHub
01 · Base & shoulder

Turn, then reach

Joystick 1 reads on A0 and A1. The base servo on D3 swings the whole turret from 0–180°. The shoulder servo on D5 lifts the upper arm between 25° and 155°.

02 · Elbow

Fold the forearm

Joystick 2 VRx on A2 drives the elbow servo on D6, limited to 20–160°. The sketch moves the angle in ±2° steps about every 20 ms while the stick is held, and holds the angle when released.

03 · Gripper

Open and close

Joystick 2 VRy on A3 controls the gripper servo on D9, 45–120°. This is the pick and place animation, simulated here, not recorded from hardware.

04 · Power

Keep servo power separate

The Uno runs from USB and feeds the joystick modules. The servos take a separate regulated supply. Join the grounds. Never connect the servo supply positive to the Uno 5V pin.
