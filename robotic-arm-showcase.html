Auto demo: the arm reaches, grips the cube, lifts, rotates, places, and releases.

Concept animation with manual servo-angle controls. This page does not connect to hardware. The camera and serial readout illustrate a planned upgrade; the joystick Arduino sketch does not implement serial control. Mechanical movement and safe angle limits require calibration on your own arm.
